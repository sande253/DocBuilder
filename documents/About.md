About the System
We are building a Document generator application, which can be used to generate documents directly instead of relying on traditional means. We use LateX to generate documents, users have the flexibility to create, edit and append the documents which were generated by our agent. 

What we are trying to solve:
In the age of AI, everybody has been using AI to create demos, presentations and even investor pitchings. But there is no presentation software which has integrated AI. If you take developer tools like Cursor, Windsurf and Kiro they are pretty wonderful and have increased the programmer's productivity by 3x, But What is there for documents in the market? are you applying for a job, you need to make the resume yourself. All these tasks are something you dont have to keep much mind into, but they are extremely tedious. This is what we are trying to automate and solve.
