\subsection{Employee Expansion}
In tandem with its financial growth, the company's workforce also experienced a significant increase. The number of employees expanded from 45 in 2020 to 60 in 2024. This growth reflects the company's expanding operations and its commitment to investing in human capital to support its strategic objectives and growing market presence.

\subsection{Customer Satisfaction Improvement}
Customer satisfaction, a critical measure of service quality and product appeal, showed substantial improvement over the five-year period. The satisfaction rating rose from 78\% in 2020 to an impressive 91\% in 2024. This upward trend indicates successful efforts in enhancing customer experience and effectively meeting client expectations.
